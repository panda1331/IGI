Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Your privacy is important to us. This page explains how we collect, store, and protect your personal data when you use our website and services.

1. What data we collect

When you book a cleaning or contact us, we may collect the following information:

  • Your name and contact details (phone number, email).
  • Your address or the address where cleaning is needed.
  • Payment information for processing your order.
  • Any messages or requests you send us.

2. How we use your data

We use your personal data only for the following purposes:

  • To provide the cleaning services you request.
  • To contact you about your order.
  • To send you reminders, invoices, and service updates.
  • To improve the quality of our services.

3. How we protect your data

We take data protection seriously. Your information is stored on secure servers and is accessible only to authorized employees. We never sell or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to your personal data.
  • Ask us to correct or delete your data.
  • Withdraw your consent at any time.
  • Contact us with any questions about your privacy.
